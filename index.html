Young Farmer Seal

An initiative by the Emirates Youth Council for Agriculture to certify and recognize young agricultural entrepreneurs.

6 Farming Categories

💧

Hydroponics

Soilless farming using nutrient-rich water solutions for efficient crop production.

🏠

Protected Agriculture

Greenhouse and controlled environment agriculture for year-round production.

🌿

Organic Farming

Chemical-free farming practices promoting soil health and biodiversity.

🌾

Traditional Agriculture

Heritage farming methods preserving UAE's agricultural traditions and knowledge.

🐄

Livestock

Animal husbandry and livestock management for sustainable food production.

🏭

Food Industries

Food processing, packaging, and value-added agricultural products.

Requirements

  • Applicant must be UAE youth (under 35 years)
  • Project must be owned and managed by youth
  • Project linked to agricultural production or food processing
  • Compliance with local regulations
  • Achieving qualifying points in technical evaluation

Certification Tiers

Category A

Beginner

  • 0-2 years experience
  • Clear operational plan
  • Eligibility for licensing support
  • Foundation to advance to Category B
Category B

Growth

  • 3-4 years experience
  • Commitment to utilizing seal benefits
  • Possibility to upgrade to Category C
Category C

Advanced

  • 5+ years experience
  • Proving innovation
  • Effective marketing plan
  • Maintaining excellence

Benefits

✓ Official recognition as a certified young farmer
✓ Increased trust and credibility in the market
✓ Enhanced visibility and exposure
✓ Partnership opportunities with leading organizations
✓ Marketing and promotional support

Agriculture Ambassadors

Empowering Emirati youth in the agricultural sector, strengthening their role in national food security, and preparing young leaders for innovation and sustainable agriculture.

About the Program

The Agriculture Ambassadors program aims to empower Emirati youth in the agricultural sector, strengthen their role in national food security, and prepare young leaders for innovation and sustainable agriculture. The program addresses youth reluctance toward agriculture and bridges the gap between research and field application.

4 Strategic Objectives

1

Qualifying national cadres in modern agriculture and food security

2

Launching innovative startups that enhance local production

3

Building partnership networks with government, private sector, and international organizations

4

Spreading awareness of sustainable agriculture and motivating youth

5 Program Pillars

01

Monthly Councils

10-12 monthly councils featuring experts in food security, smart agriculture, water management, and climate change. A platform for knowledge exchange and strategic discussions.

02

Training Workshops

6-8 workshops focused on technical and practical skills in modern agriculture. Hands-on training in cutting-edge farming technologies and sustainable practices.

03

Field Visits

4 visits to leading agricultural facilities: National Agricultural Center, Pure Harvest, Emirates Bio Farm, and Elite Agro. Real-world exposure to successful farming operations.

04

International Missions

International exposure through missions to leading agricultural nations. The Netherlands and China have shown interest, with funding being secured for these transformative experiences.

05

Agripreneur Innovation Camp

3-5 intensive days of idea generation, business model development, and investor pitches. Transform agricultural ideas into viable business ventures with expert mentorship.

UAE Youth Agriculture Community

An interactive digital platform connecting young farmers, innovators, and entrepreneurs in UAE agriculture.

About the Community

The UAE Youth Agriculture Community is an interactive digital platform on Instagram @Agriyouth.ae. It aims to attract and empower young farmers, innovators, and entrepreneurs in agriculture within the UAE. The platform works on enhancing communication, exchanging knowledge, highlighting opportunities, and supporting innovation and sustainability.

Management

Managed by the Emirates Youth Council for Agriculture, operating under the Federal Youth Authority. The community serves as the digital arm of the council's mission to engage and empower UAE youth in the agricultural sector.

What We Do

💬

Enhancing Communication

Building bridges between young agricultural professionals across the UAE.

📚

Exchanging Knowledge

Sharing expertise, research findings, and practical farming knowledge.

🎯

Highlighting Opportunities

Showcasing grants, partnerships, events, and career opportunities in agriculture.

🚀

Supporting Innovation

Fostering creative solutions and sustainable practices in UAE agriculture.
